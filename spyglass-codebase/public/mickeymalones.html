Our Story

Brockton's Corner Bar, Done Right

Since 2010, Mickey Malone's Tavern has been the South Shore's go-to for homemade food, cold pints, and every game on every screen. We're on the Stoughton line at 347 N Pearl Street — easy to find, hard to leave.

Our kitchen is built on scratch recipes. Pete's clam chowder has won awards year after year. Our pizza dough and sauce are made fresh daily, with cheese cut from 40-pound blocks. You'll taste the difference.

  • Pete's multi-award-winning New England Clam Chowder
  • Homemade pizza — dough, sauce, and real block cheese, daily
  • 12+ HD screens — Patriots, Bruins, Red Sox, UFC, all of it
  • Full kitchen until 9:45 PM, pizza & warm-ups until 11:00 PM
  • Ice-cold drafts and a full bar, every night
